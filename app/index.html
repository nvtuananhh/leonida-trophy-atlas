    The road to the platinum

    One playthrough, no online trophies, no difficulty settings. PowerPyx rates the platinum 4/10 difficulty, about 200 hours: roughly 60–80 hours of story, about 100 hours on the 100% checklist, and 10–20 hours of clean-up. The 100% checklist only appears after the story, under Progress — but everything you do during the story already counts toward it. Save at the start and end of each chapter (15 manual slots + 1 autosave; missions cannot be replayed), and never turn cheats on: cheats disable trophies and saving.

    Roadmap figures: PowerPyx — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap. powerpyx.com

    Missable — do not miss

    Only one trophy is currently flagged as potentially missable. Everything else can be finished in free roam after the story — quest givers who vanish mid-story always come back, and the checklist stays open. The one exception needs a two-minute detour in Chapter 1.

    Why the rest are safe

    Optional missions with black icons and pink letters do expire, some fences disappear for good, and a few quest lines are locked to one character (Andy is Lucia-only; Tyrone and Bret are Jason-only) — but PowerPyx confirms none of that affects trophies or 100% completion. Every trophy-related character is available again in free roam after the finale.
