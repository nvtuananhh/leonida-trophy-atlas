Roadmap to the platinum

One playthrough is enough — PowerPyx rates the platinum 4/10 and about 200 hours, with no online trophies and no difficulty settings to worry about. Cheats disable trophies, so keep the whole run cheat-free. Keep a save at the start and end of each of the five chapters: 15 manual slots plus one autosave are yours to use.

Only one trophy is even possibly missable — Positive Mental Attitude, because one meditation sits in Chapter 1 at Jason’s house. Everything else stays earnable in free-roam after the story, and the 100% checklist (Progress > Stats > 100% Completion) only opens after the credits — but everything you do during the story already counts toward it.
