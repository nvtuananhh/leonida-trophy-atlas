    The road to the platinum

    Six stages in earning order, built from the official descriptions. PowerPyx rates the set 4/10 and about 200 hours, one playthrough, no online trophies, no mission replay — so play the story first, sweep the side stories, then clean up in free-roam. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter.

    Missable — do not miss

    Only one trophy is flagged as potentially missable by the launch guides. Everything else is earned in free-roam after the story, or is an automatic story trophy. The checklist below is the whole missable watch.

    Not yet confirmed — no invented steps

    Seven trophies had no confirmed unlock method at launch (PowerPyx lists them as not yet known). Their guides in this atlas say so plainly and follow only what the official description implies. Check them again once a guide verifies the trigger in the game.

      Save habits that protect the run

      • Manual save at the start of every chapter — there is no mission replay.
      • One more manual save before the final mission.
      • Cheats disable trophies and saving (PowerPyx) — keep them off for the whole run.
